The Quiet Architecture of Digital Reading Spaces

Typography and whitespace are not mere details; they are the core foundational pillars of structural communication. When we design for long-form reading, we aren't just arranging layout grids—we are managing human attention span and eye fatigue over extended intervals.

"The best layout design is entirely invisible to the reader. They should only experience the seamless friction of the writer's thought process."

As screens evolve, our palettes must adapt. Moving away from stark, blinding pure whites (#FFFFFF) towards softer linen and slate shades allows digital books to feel organic, tactile, and anchored.
